# DRAFT client email: PP6788 first 30 days (not sent, for Sara to review)

**Subject:** Your first month of Google Ads: results so far

Hi Dr. Mistry,

Your Google Ads campaign has now been running for its first full month, so here's a quick look at how it's going.

**First 30 days (Sep 16 – Oct 15)**
- Your ads were shown 2,767 times to people searching for a chiropractor near Celina, and 128 people clicked through.
- 4.6% of people who saw your ads clicked, which is a healthy rate for a brand-new campaign.
- The ads brought in **29 phone calls**, **3 online new-patient appointment requests** and **1 contact form**.
- Investment was about $2,540, in line with the planned budget.

**What's working**
- People searching for a chiropractor in Celina and for spinal decompression are responding best. These searches are bringing in calls and bookings at the lowest cost.
- Results are improving as the campaign learns. The last week brought in nearly twice as many leads as the weekly average before it, at a lower cost per lead.
- We've added your $37 New Patient Special, same-day and walk-in appointments, insurance information, photos and links to your key pages to the ads, so people see the offer before they even click.

**One thing that would help a lot**
About 11 of the 29 calls from the ads weren't answered. These are people actively looking for a chiropractor right now, and many won't call back. Anything that helps the team catch more of these calls, or call missed numbers back quickly, will turn more of this traffic into new patients.

**Next steps on our side**
- Cleaning up searches that aren't a good fit (for example, people looking for other clinics or physical therapy) so more of the budget goes to the right people.
- Focusing the campaign on the calls and bookings that turn into new patients.
- Watching volume: the campaign could be shown more often with a larger budget. We'll share a recommendation once the cleanup has had time to settle.

Happy to walk through any of this on a call.

Sara

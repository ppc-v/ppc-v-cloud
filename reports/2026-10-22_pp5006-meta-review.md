## Findings
1. **Account authentication block.** All 16 Doc/Emergency ads went to "Update required" (error 3858385, "Please authenticate your account") at 20:36 UTC on 22 Oct. Ads still deliver, but nothing can be created or edited until someone authenticates in Ads Manager.
2. **Two ad sets with identical targeting split a small budget.** Advantage campaign budget gives Gap Free only about 20% of spend because its CPM is about 4x higher (AUD 44 vs AUD 10). Its CTR is also 4x higher (3.8% vs 1.0%), and its lead cost is as good or better: 30-day CPL 29 vs 30, 90-day CPL 28 vs 35. Gap Free is Learning Limited.
3. **One ad carries the Doc/Emergency ad set.** "Dr Tisa Kolarov" (live since June) took 67% of that ad set's 30-day spend: 90-day frequency 3.2, CTR 0.92%, CPL 35.
4. **Placements.** Over 90 days, Stories (CPL 22) and Reels (CPL 31) beat Feed (CPL 33). Reels overlay (AUD 339, CPL 48) and Notifications (AUD 142, CPL 47) were worse, and Marketplace, in-stream video, Audience Network, Instagram and Search took AUD 78 with 0 leads.
5. **Audience.** People aged 65+ took 39% of spend at CPL 29, and 25-34 had the worst CPL (75). Men had CPL 28, women CPL 45. Advantage+ audience is on, so this is information only.
6. **Lead forms not checked.** The system user has no access to the Page, so form type and questions weren't checked.
7. **Inventory flag.** The Google side is marked Canceled while Meta is still spending (the flag has been open 13 days).

## Recommendations (each needs approval before it's applied)
See the chat summary of 2026-10-22; repeated here briefly:
1. Authenticate the account in Ads Manager (needed before anything else).
2. Merge the two ad sets into one (move the Gap Free ads into Doc/Emergency, then pause the Gap Free ad set).
3. Add 2-3 new creatives: the gap-free offer with an "extras reset 31 Dec" hook, plus 9:16 versions for Stories and Reels.
4. Optional test: exclude the low-performing placements.
5. Check the lead forms: switch to "Higher intent", and ask about private health cover for gap-free leads.
6. Confirm with the client that Meta should keep running, then snooze META_ON_GOOGLE_OFF.

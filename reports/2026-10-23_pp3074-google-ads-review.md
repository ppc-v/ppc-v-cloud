## Findings
1. **Dark 23-31 Oct.** The daily budget (USD 46) plus Maximize conversions, which can spend up to 2x a day, averaged USD 65 per weekday in October. The budget was gone by the 22nd, and the rule paused the account for the last 7 weekdays.
2. **Existing patients count as leads.** "Appointment Request (Current Patient)" is a primary conversion. That's 4 of October's 13 conversions, and Maximize conversions is bidding for them. Real new-patient leads in October: 9 (USD 116 each).
3. **CPC jumped in October** (USD 27 vs 19-21) under Maximize conversions. Impression share is 21-57%, and 31-41% is lost to ad rank.
4. **One ad, every headline pinned.** Expected CTR is below average on every keyword; ad relevance is below average for "chiropractor near me" (QS 3). Ad strength is Average. There are no callout assets.
5. **Irrelevant search terms** from "chiropractor" (phrase) and the old broad keywords: dorsalgia, cause of lower back pain, back surgeries, frozen shoulder, plantar fasciitis, internal spinal stimulator, vertebral degenerative changes, back and neck massager, places to get stretched, cla insight scans, engrained chiropractic (about USD 300 over 90 days, no conversions). "quiropractico cerca" converted 2 of 2.
6. **Location settings.** Three overlapping radii (3, 6 and 8 mi, effectively 8 mi) with "presence or interest". By city: Asheville CPA about USD 86 (USD 1,604 spend); Biltmore Forest about USD 250 (USD 582).
7. **Negatives block some good searches.** "family chiropractic" (phrase) blocks "family chiropractic asheville" and "... near me", although the ads call Doster a family chiropractor. "doster" and "dr doster" exclude brand searches (fine if deliberate).
8. **April hack.** 20 rogue campaigns spent USD 2,291 in April; all removed now. Check that a refund/credit was claimed from Google and that only known users have access.
9. **Clutter.** The paused "Chiropractor" ad group has single-word broad keywords enabled ("local", "baby", "kids", "near", "close", ...). That's harmless while paused, but risky if someone switches the ad group on.

## Recommendations (each needs approval before it's applied)
1. Daily budget USD 46 → USD 33 (Google's monthly ceiling about USD 1,003). Same spend, spread across the whole month instead of stopping around the 22nd.
2. Make "Appointment Request (Current Patient)" a secondary conversion.
3. Test Maximize clicks with a USD 18 max CPC for a month, or keep Maximize conversions once goal 2 is fixed (Sara's call).
4. New RSA (unpinned, with "near me" and Merrimon Ave / North Asheville headlines) alongside the current one. Unpin most headlines in the current ad. Add callouts.
5. Add negatives (list above). Remove "family chiropractic" from the shared negatives.
6. Location: change to "presence" only and drop the 3 and 6 mi duplicates. Optional: -20% bid for Biltmore Forest.
7. Confirm the April refund and the account's user list.
8. Remove the single-word keywords in the paused "Chiropractor" ad group.

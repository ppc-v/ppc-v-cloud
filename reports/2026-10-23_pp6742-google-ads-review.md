## WhatConverts calls (50 since 27 Jul)
- 17 "No Answer" (34%), nearly all 5-15s and ending in the greeting or phone menu. Most are tagged New Patient = Yes by the menu. September's report showed 10 new-patient callers missed who never got through.
- Genuine new-patient calls (booking or real enquiry): about 9 since 27 Jul. Against about USD 8,400 spend, that's roughly USD 900+ per real new-patient call. Forms can't be measured (only a "Book Now" page view is tracked, and GA4 isn't shared with paidsearch@).
- Others: existing patients (rescheduling etc.), people looking for other businesses (e.g. Carolina Pain Center), service questions (shockwave, cold laser, kids' physicals), short IVR-only calls.
- Quotable scoring: 42 No, 3 Pending, 5 Not Set. Several clear new-patient bookings are marked No (e.g. 258412043, 261243128).

## Findings
1. **Phone handling is the biggest leak.** A third of ad calls never reach a person. Test the new-patient option in the phone menu with the client.
2. **Out of budget by mid-month.** On USD 144.25/day, Max Conv spent about USD 290/day, and the rule paused the campaign on 15 Oct. SS already lowered the budget to USD 90 on 16 Oct.
3. **Bidding on weak signals.** "New Patient Book Now Page View" (a page view, counted many per click) is primary and was 8 of October's 14 conversions. Google's "Calls from ads" is also primary (double counting). CPC rose from USD 13.68 to 25.89.
4. **Broad match.** The 3 broad keywords take about 89% of spend. Search terms include orthopedics/OrthoCarolina, pain centres, neurosurgery, other chiropractors (Twins, Harmony, Atkinson, Kuhn, Cook, Shook, NUCCA, Renoja, Vyve), dry needling, prolotherapy, shockwave, massage and products. About USD 3,700 of the USD 4,649 visible search-term spend is long tail, much of it irrelevant.
5. **Quality Score 3-4,** expected CTR below average on every keyword. All RSAs are fully pinned.

## Recommendations (each needs approval before it's applied)
1. With the client: fix/test phone answering and the IVR new-patient route (biggest win, no ad cost).
2. Make "New Patient Book Now Page View" and "Calls from ads" secondary. In WhatConverts, send only 45s+ calls (as on PP1864).
3. Manual CPC with caps (about USD 16-20), replacing Max Conversions.
4. Phrase/exact for chiropractor, chiropractor huntersville, chiropractor near me; pause the broad versions.
5. Negatives for the categories above.
6. Unpin most RSA headlines, or add an unpinned RSA per ad group.
7. Keep USD 90/day. On Manual CPC that should land close to USD 2,500/month over about 17 scheduled days; check after week 1.
8. Rescore WhatConverts calls (5 Not Set, 3 Pending, and several new-patient bookings marked No).
9. Get GA4 and GTM access to track real bookings/forms.
